## Regenerating the illustrations
```
node scripts/generate-phase-diagram.mjs                      # aidlc-phases.svg
node scripts/generate-tab-images.mjs web/image-sources/generated   # the five tab SVGs
```
Then convert each SVG to WebP at its size (quality about 90) with [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app) or any image tool, and save it into `web/public/assets/images/`. The SVGs animate their dashed arrows when opened in a browser.

## Using your own photos
Keep the theme: dark blue, abstract or technical, no vendor logos, no real faces, little embedded text.
- Crop to the size in the table, convert to WebP (quality 75 to 85) and keep each file under about 200 KB. Camera originals are often 5 to 10 MB and will slow the site.
- Check the licence and your organisation's brand guidance before using an image externally.
- Search ideas: [Unsplash: artificial intelligence](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/artificial-intelligence), [Pexels: AI software](https://www.pexels.com/search/artificial%20intelligence%20software/), [Pixabay: AI coding](https://pixabay.com/images/search/ai%20coding/).

## Folders
- `web/image-sources/originals/` holds earlier supplied images that did not fit the theme. They are not part of the site build.
- `web/image-sources/generated/` holds the editable SVG sources of the generated illustrations.
