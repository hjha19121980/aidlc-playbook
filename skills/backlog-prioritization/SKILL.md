# Backlog prioritization

Stage: Plan. Produces: `docs/aidlc/planning/prioritization-<yyyymmdd>.md`.

## Steps
1. Collect the items with value statements, estimates if available, dependencies, and deadlines.
2. Agree the method and criteria with the product owner (for example value, urgency, risk reduction, effort).
3. Score each item per criterion with a one-line justification; compute the ranking.
4. Highlight dependencies that force order and items that are cheap but valuable.
5. Present the ranked list, the assumptions, and sensitivity: what would change the order.

## Rules
- The ranking is a recommendation; the product owner decides.
- Mark inferred statements [ASSUMPTION]; keep credentials, client names, and personal data out of every artifact.
