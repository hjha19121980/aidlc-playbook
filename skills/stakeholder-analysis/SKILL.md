# Stakeholder analysis

Stage: Plan. Produces: `docs/aidlc/changes/<id>/stakeholders.md`.

## Steps
1. List people and groups affected or able to decide, by role (not personal data): users, product owner, operations, security, compliance, finance, vendors.
2. For each record interest, influence (high, medium, low), expected concern, and what they must approve.
3. Map each to a gate in the AIDLC chain (who accepts intent, spec, plan, release).
4. Propose an engagement approach: how often, which channel, who owns the relationship.
5. Flag gaps: missing roles, unclear decision rights, conflicting interests.

## Rules
- Use roles and teams, not individuals' personal details.
- Mark inferred statements [ASSUMPTION]; keep credentials, client names, and personal data out of every artifact.
