# Business case

Stage: Plan. Produces: `docs/aidlc/changes/<id>/business-case.md`.

## Steps
1. Restate the problem and goal from `intent.md`.
2. Describe two or three options, including doing nothing.
3. For each give benefits (quantified only where data exists), costs, effort range, risks, and dependencies.
4. Recommend one option and say what evidence would change the recommendation.
5. Mark every number with its source or as an assumption.

## Rules
- Do not fabricate figures. Financial content is for review by Finance before it is used externally.
- Mark inferred statements [ASSUMPTION]; keep credentials, client names, and personal data out of every artifact.
